AMINATA JABATEH / FOUNDER

Purpose meets

Aminata Jabateh is the founder of ProBrand Agency. Her work brings together an information technology background, digital business services, and nonprofit strategy. She helps individuals and organizations understand what they need to build, organize their next steps, and create practical resources and systems that support their goals.

Through ProBrand Agency, Aminata supports nonprofit funding readiness and strategy alongside digital products, digital services, and education. Her training work helps busy working parents explore how their existing knowledge can become useful digital products. These audiences have different needs, and her approach is to explain the relevant path clearly.

Based in Houston, Texas, Aminata is building ProBrand around practical guidance, clear deliverables, and sustainable growth.

THE AGENCY

One agency.
Distinct paths for your goals.

ProBrand Agency is a Houston-based agency founded by Aminata Jabateh. We support people developing digital products, businesses strengthening their online presence and systems, and nonprofits preparing for funding opportunities. Our approach starts with understanding the client’s goals, identifying what is missing, and creating a clear plan for the work ahead. Depending on the agreed service, that support may include strategy, practical resources, education, or implementation. We aim to make each engagement specific, understandable, and useful beyond the immediate project.

Our mission

Our mission is to help individuals, businesses, and nonprofits turn their knowledge and goals into practical digital products, effective digital systems, and stronger funding readiness. Through strategy, education, and hands-on support, we help clients build credibility, reach the right audience, and take informed steps toward sustainable growth.

Our vision

Our vision is a future where people can build opportunities from what they know, businesses can grow with effective digital systems, and nonprofits have the foundations to pursue funding and serve their communities sustainably.
